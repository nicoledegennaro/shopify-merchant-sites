Where Prairie Gardens is today

A proven business on aging technology.

Five stores and a distribution center have run for 35 years on an IBM AS/400 with Data Sim dumb terminals — reliable, but inventory is only seen end of day, the AS/400 expertise is retiring with Jim, and it's held together with "bubble gum and duct tape." The goal: move to a modern, PC/tablet-based system you can grow on over the next decade.

Today

Current stack

  • IBM AS/400 — POS, inventory, GL & AP (system of record)
  • Data Sim dumb terminals, batch-synced each evening
  • Platinum card processor (reliable — the bar to beat)
  • Shopify already live for gift cards · QuickBooks in the mix
Why change

The drivers

  • AS/400 knowledge is retiring; hard to hire for
  • No live inventory — buyers decide on end-of-day numbers
  • Want to modernize and keep e-commerce, BOPIS, wholesale open
  • A real loyalty program ("I Belong") instead of a name only
Keep in mind

Must-handle details

  • Sets — buy a 3-pack, sell singly, track each (~2–3k SKUs)
  • Distribution center + direct-to-store receiving
  • Floral design — raw stems → finished arrangements
  • Markdowns, ad/sale files, buyer reports

The honest framing: Shopify won't replace the AS/400 100% on day one — it covers roughly 80% natively, with apps and a QuickBooks connector filling the rest. The aim tomorrow isn't a line-by-line bake-off against Epicor; it's a clear picture of how the pieces fit, what each plan costs at your volume, and whether the card rate is competitive.

Why move processing to Shopify Payments

  • No more finger-pointing — one vendor for POS + payments, nothing to reconcile between systems at day's end
  • Rates published and negotiable at $16M
  • Still accept card, debit, check, and cash at the counter

Or keep Platinum

  • Supported, but an outside processor usually means manual entry + end-of-day reconciliation between the terminal and Shopify
  • You lose the single-vendor simplicity above
  • We'll only recommend switching if the rate + reliability genuinely win
