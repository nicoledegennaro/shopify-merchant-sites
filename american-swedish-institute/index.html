The Case for Change

Why now is the right window to consolidate on a modern commerce platform.

ASI's mission runs across a museum gift shop, an online store, and a member + donor community that all need to share one customer record. Today, those touchpoints typically run on legacy or stitched-together tools — making every campaign, every refund, and every event harder than it should be.

01

Legacy gift-shop POS is a ceiling

Older POS systems are expensive to maintain, slow at the counter, and don't share inventory or customer data with the website. Replacing them with a modern, cloud-native POS removes a daily friction tax on staff and volunteers.

02

Fragmented donor / member / shopper record

When the same person buys a ticket, renews a membership, and orders a book online, those should be one profile — not three. A unified commerce platform makes loyalty, segmentation, and stewardship dramatically easier.

03

Seasonality demands reliability

Holiday markets, FIKA café traffic, and Midsommar all spike traffic in narrow windows. A platform that's throughput-ready and battle-tested at Black Friday scale removes the risk of outages exactly when revenue matters most.

Other cultural institutions run on Shopify precisely because it gives them enterprise-grade reliability at a price point a museum gift shop can sustain.
The Bottom Line

A right-sized stack: Shopify Advanced + POS Pro.

For ASI's footprint, the recommendation is Shopify Advanced at a negotiated $67/month TOS deal rate (vs. $399 list) plus POS Pro ($89/location/month) — not Shopify Plus. This pairing gives ASI the full unified-commerce stack at under $2K/year in platform fees, before processing.

Value Beyond Cost

What ASI gets that a typical "cheaper" stack can't deliver.

Platform cost is only the entry ticket. The bigger value lives in unified commerce, modern checkout, and the operational lift staff and volunteers get back every day.

Shop Pay & modern checkout

Shop Pay is the highest-converting checkout on the internet. For a museum gift shop, that translates to fewer abandoned carts on holiday traffic and more first-time visitors becoming repeat buyers.

Why it matters: even a small lift on online conversion compounds into real dollars across a 3-year window.

Unified commerce: one customer record

Membership purchases, café/restaurant retail, online orders, and event ticketing-adjacent merchandise can all live against one customer profile — enabling cleaner segmentation, smarter email, and better donor stewardship.

Why it matters: the same visitor who buys a book on-site shouldn't be a stranger when they shop online next week.

Throughput-ready POS

Shopify POS handles holiday-market and Black Friday throughput at global scale — Shopify processed over $14B GMV on BFCM weekend, with peak volumes well beyond anything a museum gift shop will see. ASI inherits that reliability by default.

Why it matters: the register doesn't slow down during Julmarknad.

App + integration ecosystem

7,000+ apps in the Shopify App Store plus enterprise-grade APIs mean almost every donor CRM, email platform, membership tool, and accounting system already has a turnkey connection.

Why it matters: donor CRMs and membership tools (Salesforce, DonorPerfect, Bloomerang, Mailchimp) plug in without a custom build.
