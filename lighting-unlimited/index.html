Overview

Where Shopify fits

Lighting Unlimited is a lighting & electrical distributor serving the greater Phoenix Valley from four branches, with roughly 92% of sales B2B. The opportunity isn't a new sales team — it's giving the customers you already serve a faster way to buy.

The problem today

Nearly every order runs through a person — walk-ins, phone calls, and manually written tickets on a pre-coded pricing matrix. It works, but as the company scales, the overhead to handle all those manual transactions gets heavy. There's no online interface for the customers who just want to log in and re-order at their agreed pricing.

The goal

A branded, gated storefront (e.g. an LU-owned domain "powered by Shopify") where account customers log in to their own tier pricing and self-serve — while DDI stays your source of truth and the big, complex spec jobs stay exactly where they are.

Guardrail we'll respect: manufacturers (Acuity, Philips, Lutron, Minka, etc.) restrict public online listing. Everything sits behind a login on your own branded site — not on a public marketplace — which keeps you inside your buying-group and channel agreements.
Current State

How it works right now

A quick map of today's stack and the three ways customers buy — so we only rebuild what's worth rebuilding.

Systems today

  • ERP: DDI (distributor ERP) — stays the system of record; you may evaluate NetSuite down the line
  • E-commerce: none currently (previous site retired); manual listings on Amazon & eBay, low volume
  • Payments: a separate card processor (buying-group negotiated rates); most sales are on terms / AR
  • Buying group: Affiliated Distributors — channel & pricing agreements to preserve

Three ways customers buy

  • Counter & pop-up pricing — walk-in / phone, pre-coded tier pricing. ← the best fit to move online
  • Outside sales / spec jobs — complex quotes, large POs (e.g. $100k retrofits). Stays in DDI
  • Municipal / contract — blanket POs & pre-negotiated annual pricing; one city requires an online PO portal

Pricing complexity

Tiers today are bronze / silver / gold, moving toward a cleaner bronze / silver / gold / platinum model — with customer-category modifiers (electrician vs. general contractor vs. property management). Net terms (net 30 / 45 / 60) are common. This tier count is the main thing that decides the plan (below).
