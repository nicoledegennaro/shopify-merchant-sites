Why Plus wins anyway

What you want to do is Plus-only.

Per-customer & volume pricing

Chemical is priced per customer by volume. Advanced gives one storefront price (your single contractor tier). Per-customer catalogs & price lists are Plus B2B — the unlock to sell chemical online at all.

Self-serve net terms

Let a customer with a clean balance order on account at 6:30pm without calling their rep. Native on Plus B2B. On Advanced, terms only work by manually building each order — the opposite of self-serve.

Checkout extensibility

Cart upsells and add-to-cart AOV boosters you asked about — Plus only.

Account team + Solutions Engineer

Dedicated pre/post-launch support — directly useful for the Business Central + Salesforce work.

Higher API rate limits

Matters for reliable Business Central + Salesforce integrations at order volume.

3 months free

Magento → Plus migration promo applies to you — offsets most of year one.

  • Checkout extensibility: overview.
