Advanced vs Plus for B2B

Where Advanced ends — and Plus begins.

As of April 2026, Advanced includes native B2B — company accounts, up to 3 catalogs, volume pricing, net terms, and self-serve ordering. So the question isn't "can we do B2B" — it's whether your pricing outgrows Advanced's limits. For chemical, it likely does.

What Advanced now covers

Company accounts, up to 3 active B2B catalogs (assigned via Markets), volume/quantity pricing, net payment terms, and self-serve checkout on account. Genuinely capable for a handful of shared tiers.

Where you cross into Plus

More than 3 catalogs or per-account negotiated pricing; catalogs assigned directly to a company/location (not routed through Markets); and deposits, partial payments, pay-per-fulfillment.

Your chemical case → Plus

You said every chemical customer is priced differently by volume. If that's per-account pricing (many distinct price lists), Advanced's 3-catalog cap breaks immediately — which puts you on Plus.

Also Plus

Dedicated account team + Solutions Engineer (useful for the BC + Salesforce work), higher API rate limits for those integrations, richest checkout extensibility, and the 3-months-free Magento promo.

The real qualifying question: how many distinct price lists do you need, and is pricing negotiated per account or by shared volume tiers? Three or fewer shared tiers + net terms = Advanced can do it. Per-account pricing or 4+ lists = Plus. (One check: confirm B2B is switched on in your admin — the non-Plus rollout reached select plans.)

The deciding question

Does chemical need per-customer pricing?

As of April 2026, Advanced already includes B2B — company accounts with approval, up to 3 catalogs, volume pricing, net terms, and self-serve ordering. So most of what you described runs on Advanced. Plus is only required for a few specific things.

Advanced can do it if…

  • Chemical priced in 3 or fewer shared volume tiers
  • Accounts approved before they see pricing/order
  • Self-serve order placement + net terms
  • Phase-1 flow: customer places order → you set shipping & final total → they pay via Shopify

You need Plus if…

  • Per-customer pricing — every account priced differently (more than 3 price lists)
  • Catalog assigned directly to a company/location
  • Deposits or partial payments on an order
So the one answer that picks the plan: is chemical priced per account (→ Plus) or in ≤3 volume tiers (→ Advanced, and you save ~$22K/yr)?
Your questions, answered

Integrations & self-serve.

Can BC + Salesforce integrations be added on Advanced AND Plus?

Yes — both. The Business Central connector and Salesforce sync aren't plan-gated. Plus adds higher API rate limits and a dedicated team for the build, but the connections work on either plan.

Do we need Plus for customer self-serve order placement?

No. Self-serve B2B ordering, approval-gated company accounts, and net terms are on Advanced (since Apr 2026). Plus is only for per-customer pricing (>3 catalogs), deposits, or partial payments.

Order on account / net terms with the BC net-balance check?

Net terms themselves are on Advanced. The live BC credit-balance check at checkout is a custom integration either way — a phase-2 build. Start with the "place order → you finalize shipping/total → pay via Shopify" flow (works on both plans).

What about the chemical approval gate?

Handled by only granting approved companies the chemical catalog — so a customer can't self-create an account and instantly get chemical pricing. Works on both plans.
