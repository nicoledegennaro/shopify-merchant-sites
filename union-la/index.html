Getting started

How to switch Shopify Payments back on.

You've done this once already, so most of your details are on file — reactivation takes minutes from your Shopify admin. Here's the full flow, and the one step that actually waives the third-party fee.

Before you start — have these ready

  • Business bank account in the US (for payouts)
  • Two-step authentication enabled on the account
  • Legal business name, address, and business type
  • Tax ID (EIN)
  • Authorized representative's details + government ID
  • Bank routing and account numbers
1

Open Settings → Payments

In your Shopify admin, click Settings, then Payments. Shopify Payments sits at the top; your third-party gateway shows as the active provider.

⬡ Shopify Payments Shop Pay, Apple Pay & Google Pay
Activate
▤ Third-party gateway active
Manage
2

Activate Shopify Payments

Click Activate Shopify Payments (you may see "Switch to Shopify Payments" coming from another gateway). This reopens the onboarding form — pre-filled from your fall 2025 activation.

3

Confirm business & representative details

Verify your business type, legal name, address, and tax ID, then the authorized representative's details. Most of this should already be saved from last year.

4

Set statement descriptor & payout schedule

Choose how Union LA appears on customers' card statements, and set your payout schedule — daily, weekly, or monthly.

5

Confirm your payout bank account

Verify the business bank account where Shopify deposits your payouts.

6

Submit for verification

Save to submit. Shopify verifies your details, occasionally requesting a document. Make sure two-step authentication is on, or payouts can be paused.

7

Deactivate the third-party gateway — this waives the fee

Back on Settings → Payments, manage the third-party provider and deactivate it so Shopify Payments is your sole card gateway. This is the step that removes the 0.2% third-party transaction fee (~$4.4K/yr).

⬡ Shopify Payments Active · sole card gateway
Manage
▤ Third-party gateway Third-party provider
Deactivate
💡 Until the third-party gateway is off, the 0.2% fee can still apply — don't skip this step (it's the one that got missed last time).
8

Confirm Shop Pay & place a test order

Shop Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay switch on automatically with Shopify Payments. Run a test order to confirm checkout and your first payout — and you're live.
