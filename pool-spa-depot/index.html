Built For Specialty Retail

Why Shopify for Pool & Spa Depot

A unified platform purpose-built for retailers running brick-and-mortar and high-ticket e-commerce side by side.

01

One Backend, Store + Online

A single source of truth across all 9 locations and your e-commerce site. Inventory, customers, and orders live in one system — no middleware, no nightly syncs, no data lag.

02

Real-Time Multi-Location Inventory

Show accurate stock at every store, not just the warehouse. Enables ship-from-store, buy-online-pickup-in-store, and accurate availability for serialized products — natively.

03

Built for High-Ticket Conversion

Shop Pay accelerates checkout with industry-leading conversion lift. Shopify Installments offers BNPL for $15K–$20K products, keeping high-AOV customers from dropping off.

04

Serialized Inventory, Native

Track individual units by serial number end-to-end — purchase, delivery, warranty registration, and service history. Critical for hot tubs, saunas, cold plunges, and above-ground pools.

05

AI Commerce & Discoverability

Native ChatGPT shopping integration, schema-rich product data for search visibility, and Shopify Magic to help your team produce product content at scale.

06

Built to Scale Nationally

When you're ready to expand beyond KY/TN/AL, the same platform handles multi-region tax, shipping zones, currency, and international storefronts. No re-platforming required.
