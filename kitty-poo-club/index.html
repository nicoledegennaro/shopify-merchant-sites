Subscriptions on Shopify.

Yes — your subscriptions run on Shopify Payments. You keep your subscription app; only the processor changes underneath it.

What actually changes

Your subscription app (e.g., Recharge) and all your contracts stay exactly as they are. The only change is the processor behind the rebill — Stripe → Shopify Payments. This is the same setup 1st Phorm runs.

How the migration works

Saved card tokens transfer processor-to-processor — customers never re-enter payment info. It's phased: new subscriptions route to Shopify Payments first, existing tokens move in the background.

Why it's cash-flow-safe

  • No rebill gap. Because tokens are migrated (not re-collected), recurring charges keep running without interruption — 1st Phorm's token move happened overnight with no churn spike.
  • Lower involuntary churn over time. Network tokenization + automatic card-updater keep subscriber cards current, so renewals don't fail when a card expires.
  • Cleaner reconciliation. One payout stream, easier to reconcile than a gateway-plus-processor stack.
Two things to confirm so there's no surprise: (1) first-payout timing on a new Shopify Payments account vs. your current Stripe cadence, and (2) that no rolling reserve is applied — unlikely given your volume and clean chargeback history, but we'll get underwriting to confirm.
Bottom line: bringing subscriptions over is operationally cash-flow-safe. Whether it's cash-flow-positive comes down to the rate — which is the next tab.
