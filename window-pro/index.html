Why Shopify

Modern, fast to launch, low lift

80/20

About 80% of your store is ready out-of-the-box — theme, checkout, payments, Shop Pay, and apps. You focus on the 20% that's unique to Window Pro: your surplus catalog, local pickup, and the install add-on.

◆

App ecosystem — no custom build needed

Thousands of apps cover what you need: local pickup/delivery (e.g. Zapiet), install/service add-ons & product options, marketplace sync (Facebook), reviews, and email/SMS marketing.

⚡

ROI with Shop Pay + the Shop app

Shop Pay one-click checkout lifts conversion (and Shop Pay Installments helps on higher-ticket units); the Shop app puts your products in front of millions of shoppers with built-in order tracking. For surplus/dead stock, even modest recovery outweighs the platform cost many times over.
