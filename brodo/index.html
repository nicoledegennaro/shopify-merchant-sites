Addressing Your Team's Key Questions
❑ "It looks extremely expensive, especially subscription fees (e.g., Recharge)"
● You won't need Recharge — the fee you're worried about is avoidable. You're on WooCommerce Subscriptions today (effectively free). Shopify's native Subscriptions works the same way — it creates the recurring order and charges through your processor, with no separate per-transaction app fee. (For reference: Recharge Pro runs $499/mo + 1.34% + 19¢/transaction — exactly the per-order fee you want to steer clear of, and you can.)
● The real test is capability, not cost. The open question is whether native Subscriptions (or a low-cost app like Loop/Skio) handles your heavier modifications — swaps, add/remove, frequency changes, pauses, and post-purchase/cancellation surveys — as robustly as your custom Woo setup. That's what we'll validate together before anything else.
● TCO is the right lens — your Woo stack carries hosting, plugin licenses, developer maintenance, and PCI overhead that Shopify consolidates into one platform fee.
● Payment rates are negotiable on Plus, with no third-party transaction fee on Shopify Payments — often net-comparable or better than WooPay/Stripe once conversion is factored in.
● We'll build a side-by-side TCO with your real numbers so it's not guesswork.
❑ "It's hard to assess claims of improved conversion"
● Shopify Checkout processes ~10% of US e-commerce — conversion figures are observed at scale, not estimates.
● Shop Pay's 100M+ network is the differentiator — one-tap reorders for a repeat-purchase brand like Brodo. WooPay can't match the network.
● Speed drives conversion — moving mobile LCP from ~4.2s to <2.5s is a lift before checkout even starts.
● We'll benchmark your current checkout rate and model the lift on comparable food/subscription brands.
❑ "Transition work, risk, and cost"
● Plus includes a Launch Engineer + Merchant Success Manager through the whole migration.
● Woo→Shopify is one of the most common migrations — mature tools for data, subscriptions, and SEO (301s, URL mapping).
● Phased migration is an option (parallel run / storefront-first).
● The cost of staying compounds — every WP update, plugin conflict, and security patch is ongoing risk Shopify removes.
❑ "We get extraordinary service from Woo's concierge program"
● Dedicated Merchant Success Manager — a strategic partner, not a ticket queue.
● 24/7 priority support for Plus merchants.
● Plus Academy, exclusive betas, and merchant community, with access to product teams.
● As a premium food/CPG subscription brand, Brodo is exactly who Plus invests in — happy to connect you with peers who've migrated.
