Grow vs. Plus — what the upgrade unlocks

You're already on Shopify. As you approach ~$2.4M in annual sales, here's an honest look at the cost difference — and why Plus pays for itself through growth, not fees.

The honest cost picture

Grow vs. Plus at ~$2.4M / year

Plus has a lower card rate (2.25% vs 2.7%), but a higher platform fee. Here's how it nets out on ~$2.4M of online card volume. (The 30¢ per-transaction fee is identical on both, so it's left out.)

 GrowPlus
Platform fee / year $948 ($79/mo) $27,600 ($2,300/mo)
Online card rate 2.7% + 30¢ 2.25% + 30¢
Processing on $2.4M $64,800 $54,000
All-in / year $65,748 $81,600
The lower rate saves ~$10,800/yr on processing — but Plus's platform fee is ~$26,700 higher, so on fees alone Plus costs about $15,850 more per year at $2.4M. On card rate alone, Plus doesn't break even until roughly $5.9M/yr. So the real question isn't "is Plus cheaper" — it's "can Plus grow revenue by more than ~$16K?" For a repeat-order food brand like yours, that bar is low.
Where Plus pays for itself

Three Plus features built for how you sell

You sell fresh, day-based menus and weekly market boxes to a repeat audience. These three Plus-only capabilities map directly to that — and any one of them on its own more than covers the ~$16K premium.

Plus only · Checkout Extensibility

Custom checkout + one-click upsells

Add "make it a meal" upsells right in the checkout — a dessert, a side, a drink, an extra box — plus custom delivery-day/window rules so orders come in clean. Plus unlocks the deep checkout customization that Grow can't do.

Why it works for Kinney Lane: your customers are already buying food they love — a $6–10 add-on at the moment of purchase is the easiest yes in the business, and it lifts every single order.

+$48,000/yr illustrative: a conservative +2% AOV lift on $2.4M in orders
Plus only · Launchpad

Automated weekly menu drops

Schedule your MON/WED menus, ordering windows, price changes, and promos to go live automatically — no one staying up to flip products on and off. Launchpad runs your recurring drops for you.

Why it works for Kinney Lane: your menu changes on a rhythm. Automating each drop means orders open on time every time, you capture peak-demand windows, and your team stops doing manual release work every week.

+$24,000/yr illustrative: +1% revenue from on-time drops + reclaimed staff hours
Plus only · Shopify Audiences

Cheaper new-customer acquisition

Audiences uses buying signals from across the Shopify network to build high-intent custom audiences you push straight to Meta, Google, TikTok, and more — so your ad dollars reach people far more likely to order.

Why it works for Kinney Lane: a new meal-box customer isn't a one-time sale — they reorder week after week. Acquiring them more efficiently compounds, because every first order you win at a lower cost becomes repeat revenue. (Requires Shopify Payments; US/Canada.)

+$24,000/yr illustrative: more first orders at the same ad spend from higher-ROAS targeting
