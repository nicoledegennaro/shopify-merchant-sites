Value beyond cost

What the TCO above doesn't capture.

Shop Pay conversion lift

Plus customers see 1–3% AOV lift on average from Shop Pay's accelerated checkout. At $50M GMV, even a 1% lift is +$500K/yr in revenue. Not modeled in the TCO above — pure upside.

Audiences + Functions + Flow

Plus-only marketing and automation tools. Audiences improves paid acquisition ROAS by 10–25%; Functions powers custom commerce logic (Catbird's configurators); Flow automates back-office workflows that currently burn dev hours.

Reduced agency dependency

Plus customers reduce external dev agency spend post-launch as their teams take more ownership through the Shopify admin. Conservatively modeled as a 75% reduction above; many customers see 90%+.

Time-to-market on new features

Catbird's marketing team can ship promotions, A/B tests, and campaigns without engineering tickets. The Magento "everything needs a dev" tax goes away. Hard to put a dollar number on it — easy to feel.

Why Shopify Plus

The reasons that matter for Catbird specifically.

  • Configurator-native architecture. Confetti + Memento + engraving all map cleanly to metaobjects, metafields, and Cart Transform Functions — what Catbird is custom-building today on Magento.
  • NetSuite + SuperSync integration is well-trodden. SuperSync ↔ Shopify is a documented path. The integration risk is low.
  • Plus card rates beat Braintree at scale. Plus standard 2.25% vs. typical Magento Braintree 2.5%+ saves 25–50bps on $50M GMV.
  • Marketing team self-service. Promotions, audiences, content updates without dev tickets.
  • Klaviyo + Attentive + Yotpo + Kustomer all integrate cleanly. Catbird's existing martech stack stays. No forced replacements.
  • PE-friendly cost structure. Predictable license + processing line that scales with revenue. No surprise infrastructure bills.
  • Faster time-to-launch than re-platforming Magento again. Lazer's 17-week build is on the short end of comparable enterprise migrations.
  • Account team + dedicated support. Plus includes account-managed commercial support through migration and beyond.
