Why Shopify Plus

The reasons that matter for Catbird specifically.

  • Configurator-native architecture. Confetti + Memento + engraving map cleanly to metaobjects, metafields, and Cart Transform Functions — what Catbird is custom-building today on Magento.
  • NetSuite + SuperSync is well-trodden. SuperSync ↔ Shopify is a documented path. Integration risk is low.
  • Plus card rates beat Braintree at scale. 2.25% vs. typical Magento Braintree 2.5%+ saves 25–50bps on $50M GMV.
  • Marketing team self-service. Promotions, audiences, content updates without dev tickets.
  • Existing stack stays. Klaviyo + Attentive + Yotpo + Kustomer all integrate cleanly. No forced replacements.
  • PE-friendly cost structure. Predictable license + processing that scales with revenue. No surprise infrastructure bills.
  • Faster time-to-launch than re-platforming Magento again. Lazer's 17-week build is on the short end of comparable enterprise migrations.
  • Account-managed support. Plus includes dedicated commercial support through migration and beyond.
