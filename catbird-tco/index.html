What the 0.30% approval means

We secured online VPF approval at 0.30% — a 0.05-point cut from the 0.35% standard, or roughly 14% off your variable platform fee. Because VPF is charged on GMV, the dollar savings grow every year Catbird grows. This approval is valid through July 30, 2026.

Live VPF calculator

Variable Platform Fee — at any GMV.

Plus charges the higher of two amounts: a flat base license ($2,300/month) or a percentage of monthly GMV (0.30% — Catbird's approved online rate, down from the 0.35% standard). At low GMV the base applies. As revenue grows, VPF takes over. Adjust the inputs to see how the math plays out at any volume.

$10M+ annual · Catbird's band
VPF scales linearly
At $50M: $150K/yr. At $100M: $300K/yr. Rate stays at 0.30% no matter how big.
Why this is good news for Catbird: the rate stays at 0.30% (Catbird's approved online rate, down from the 0.35% standard) as Catbird scales. You pay more in absolute dollars because you're selling more — but the percentage never moves against you. Most enterprise platforms scale their cut as you grow. Plus doesn't.
