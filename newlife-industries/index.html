Your stack, connected

The new store plugs into the systems you already run — keeping your paperless, automated workflow intact.

ERP · CRM · WMS

NetSuite

NetSuite is one of the most common ERPs among Shopify merchants. A direct connector (e.g., Celigo or the NetSuite Connector) syncs orders, inventory, customers and fulfillment automatically — so the new store feeds NetSuite the same paperless way your wholesale sites do.

Email marketing

Klaviyo

Native Shopify integration. Sync your 125k customer base, build segments, and trigger flows — so you can market the new retail brand to the audience you already own.

Shipping

ShipStation

Native integration pulls orders for labels and fulfillment — supporting the fast 2–3 day turnaround the retail buyer expects.

Reach

Social & marketplaces

Built-in channels for Facebook & Instagram, Google, and the Shop app (200M+ shoppers) — the reason Shopify fits the social-first retail push.
