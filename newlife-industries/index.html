Your stack, connected

The new store plugs into the systems you already run — keeping your paperless, automated workflow intact.

ERP · CRM · WMS

NetSuite

NetSuite is one of the most common ERPs among Shopify merchants. A direct connector (e.g., Celigo or the NetSuite Connector) syncs orders, inventory, customers and fulfillment automatically — so the new store feeds NetSuite the same paperless way your wholesale sites do.

Email marketing

Klaviyo

Native Shopify integration. Sync your 125k customer base, build segments, and trigger flows — so you can market the new retail brand to the audience you already own.

Shipping

ShipStation

Native integration pulls orders for labels and fulfillment — supporting the fast 2–3 day turnaround the retail buyer expects.

Reach

Social & marketplaces

Built-in channels for Facebook & Instagram, Google, and the Shop app (200M+ shoppers) — the reason Shopify fits the social-first retail push.

Room to grow

The retail store is the starting point. As it proves out, the same platform can carry more of NewLife.

Multi-brand

All five brands, one home

Run each brand as its own storefront under a single Shopify organization — one login, one place to see every order across the umbrella.

Wholesale

B2B on the same platform

Your volume/wholesale model (Workplace Pro and the catalog brands) can live on native Shopify B2B — company accounts, custom pricing, and bulk ordering, still synced to NetSuite.

Simplicity

One admin, less overhead

Ready-to-go storefronts instead of building each site from scratch — lower cost to stand up new brands as you expand.

Audience

Reach where buyers are

Social channels and the Shop app put your one-off retail products in front of individual shoppers — the audience the wholesale sites were never built for.
