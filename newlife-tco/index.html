3 · What Plus can earn back — the uplift levers

Plus isn't a cheaper checkout — it's a bigger checkout. Here's each Plus-only lever in dollars, modeled on your conservative $300K Year-1 base. Illustrative to show the mechanism — swap in your real numbers.

AOV · Checkout extensibility

Bigger average order

Upsells and personalization add-ons built into checkout — "add a matching tote," "customize a second item." Checkout upsells commonly lift AOV 5–10%; fits your bundle model (free personalization at 6+, free shipping at 12+).

+5% AOV → +$15,000 / yr
Acquisition · Shopify Audiences

Sharper reactivation, lower CAC

High-intent target lists from Shop-app data → Google/Meta for the ~20K below-minimum buyers you're pushing to the store. You only pay when they convert. Model: a 10% conversion lift on ~$150K of social-driven revenue.

+10% on social-driven revenue → +$15,000 / yr
Conversion · Shop Pay

Fewer abandoned carts

Full checkout extensibility + Shop Pay's one-tap checkout lifts completion — meaningful on cold social traffic that bounces easily. Model: +1.5% conversion on the $300K base.

+1.5% conversion → +$4,500 / yr
Speed-to-value · Account team

Protects the launch season

Me + Angel + a CSM: launch review, NetSuite / Klaviyo / ShipStation integration guidance, post-launch optimization. Hitting late-August means catching the full back-to-school window instead of slipping ~4 weeks into it.

~4 weeks of season protected → ~$30,000 (one-time)

Scale the recurring three (AOV + Audiences + Shop Pay) to $1M GMV and they run ~$115,000/yr on their own. The bigger the store gets, the more lopsided this becomes.

5 · Recommendation — start on Plus

The goal you described is a growth play, and growth lives on Plus.

The real prize you laid out is reactivating 40K+ below-minimum buyers and pushing this new arm toward 7 figures. That's an acquisition + average-order-value game — and the two tools built for exactly that, Shopify Audiences and checkout extensibility, are Plus-only. Launching on Advanced means launching without the levers that drive the outcome you're after.

Cost isn't the decision here — the upside is.

Yes, Plus carries a higher fixed cost early (~$22K/yr at $300K). But that gap is small against the prize: it's covered by a 4% GMV uplift at $1M and just 1% at $3M — well within reach of the Audiences + AOV levers once you're driving traffic to the store. Add the dedicated team (me + Angel + a CSM) making sure you hit the late-August date and integrate NetSuite / Klaviyo / ShipStation cleanly, and Plus stops being a cost line and becomes the engine for the growth you're building.

Bottom line: Plus from day one — so the tools that grow the base are in place before the traffic arrives, not bolted on after.
