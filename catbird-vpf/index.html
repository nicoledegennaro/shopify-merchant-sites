The rate grows with you. VPF is a percentage of GMV, so the 0.30% saving isn't a fixed discount — it scales as Catbird grows. Every additional $10M in annual GMV widens the gap by another $5,000/yr vs. the standard 0.35%. Your peak months (Nov–Dec) already save nearly $9K between them.

How VPF works: each month you pay the greater of the $2,300 base or 0.30% × that month's GMV. At Catbird's volume, VPF exceeds the base every month (the base only applies below ~$767K/month), so every month is billed at the rate. GMV figures are your last-12-months online totals (~$50.9M); the 0.35% column is what the same volume would have cost at the standard rate. Approved 0.30% rate valid through July 30, 2026.
