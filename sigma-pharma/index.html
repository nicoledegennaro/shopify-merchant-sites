Plan: Shopify Plus Category: Restricted — wholesale pharma distribution Partner: Simplistic Sigma lead: Nichole Moreau
Where we are

Two tracks, running in parallel.

Sigma is a wholesale pharmaceutical distributor moving onto Shopify Plus. Because pharma is a regulated category, two things run side by side: (1) eligibility — clearing Shopify's Restricted Categories Program — and (2) the build — scoping the storefront with Simplistic. Neither has to wait entirely on the other, but program approval is the gate to go live.

✓ Momentum

Discovery done, supportability review underway, and the Due Diligence Questionnaire (DDQ) is complete and being submitted to the program.

🔑 The gate

Approval into the Restricted Categories Program — driven by the DDQ, accreditation/licensing, and supportability review. Spots are limited, so timing matters.

🚀 In parallel

Kick off scoping with Simplistic so the build is ready to move the moment eligibility clears — no time lost.
