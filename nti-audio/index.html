Overview

The three Shopify capabilities that solve this for you.

Your goal — one store, every market, with each subsidiary still its own business — maps cleanly to three things Shopify Plus does natively. Quick education before the options.

Shopify Markets

Sell to the whole world from a single store. Markets automatically localizes currency, pricing, taxes & duties, and language by country, with region-specific domains — one catalog, one admin.

For NTi: this is how "one unified store" actually works — customers land in their own market, and you add new countries as you expand worldwide without rebuilding.

Multi-entity selling

Run multiple legal entities inside one store. Each entity invoices under its own name, with its own payouts, bank account, and tax registration — routed automatically by market.

For NTi: the exact piece you asked about — NTi US, UK, FR & DE each keep their own invoicing, shipping, and banking, all within a single storefront.

Shop Pay & Shopify Payments

The world's best-converting checkout — up to ~50% higher conversion vs. guest checkout — plus the lowest card rates of any plan and one processor across every market.

For NTi: you're not on it today, so it's immediate growth on the US store and consolidated payments as you scale (and it pairs with Plus B2B for your 90–95% B2B mix).
All three are included on Shopify Plus — $2,300/mo, the foundation under every option on the next tab.
The decision

Three ways to structure it.

All three run on Shopify Plus and cost the same — $2,300/mo (you're well under the variable-fee threshold, so it's the flat base). The choice isn't about price; it's about how you want to operate.

Recommended

1 · One unified store — Shopify Markets + multi-entity

A single store and admin that adapts to each market automatically.

What it is

  • Markets auto-adapts currency, pricing & tax by country — customers land on their region.
  • Multi-entity lets each subsidiary (NTi US, UK, FR, DE) invoice under its own legal entity, with its own payouts & bank account.
  • Per-region inventory & shipping origins via store locations + fulfillment routing.

Why this fits you

  • It's exactly what you described — one store, region-aware, with each subsidiary still owning invoicing/shipping/banking.
  • One admin to manage and the lowest overhead.
  • The cleanest base to add markets as you expand worldwide.
Pricing: Shopify Plus $2,300/mo — one store. Markets & multi-entity included; no per-store fees. (Flat base — your ~$400K GMV is well under the variable-fee threshold.)

2 · Expansion stores — a separate store per subsidiary

Each subsidiary runs its own full storefront, admin & team.

What it is

  • Each region is its own Shopify store (separate admin, theme, catalog), grouped under one Plus organization.

Why / when it fits

  • Only if subsidiaries want fully independent storefronts, teams, and logins rather than one shared store.
  • More autonomy per region — but more to manage (multiple admins, themes, catalogs).
Pricing: Shopify Plus $2,300/mo — includes up to 9 expansion stores, covering your 5–7 footprint at no extra cost (beyond 9 it's $250/mo each).

3 · Two-store fallback — US + one HQ-run EU store

Keep the US store; consolidate all of Europe into one HQ-run store.

What it is

  • US stays its own store; a single European store run by Swiss HQ handles all EU invoicing & shipping — no per-subsidiary integration.

Why / when it fits

  • The simplest path if full multi-entity unification is more than you want to take on this year.
  • Fewer moving parts — but less granular per-country control, and HQ owns EU operations.
Pricing: Shopify Plus $2,300/mo — two stores, within the included expansion stores.

Payments

Turn on Shopify Payments — unlock Shop Pay & the lowest rates.

You're not on Shopify Payments today. Switching is where a lot of the growth upside lives — and it's the same processor across every market.

Shop Pay — the world's best-converting checkout. Plus customers see up to ~50% higher conversion vs. guest checkout. For a growth-focused store, that's direct revenue.
Lowest card rates of any plan on Plus: 2.15% + 30¢ (Visa/Mastercard), 3.15% + 30¢ (Amex / international) — negotiable at volume.
Per-entity payouts & bank accounts. With multi-entity, each subsidiary gets its own payouts and bank account — all from one store.
One processor across markets = consolidated reconciliation and far less admin than separate processors per region.
Built for your B2B mix. Since 90–95% of your business is B2B, Shop Pay + Plus B2B (company accounts, payment terms) streamlines repeat and trade ordering too.
No third-party gateway fees when Shopify Payments is your sole processor (otherwise Plus adds 0.20%/transaction).
