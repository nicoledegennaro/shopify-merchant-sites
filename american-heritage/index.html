Advanced is locked into the standard Shopify checkout shell. Plus opens the checkout up as an extensible surface — you can collect the information you actually need to enroll a student, run a program, and book a fundraising donation in the same flow. This matters more for a school than for almost any other category, because the cart-to-enrollment hand-off today is where data gets dropped.

Student-Specific Fields

Capture student name, grade level, age/date of birth, and current school at checkout, tied directly to the order — not in a separate post-purchase form or spreadsheet. Group/family enrollments can list multiple students in a single transaction.

Cleaner enrollment data · fewer support tickets

Parent / Guardian + Emergency Contact

Required parent/guardian fields, secondary contact, emergency contact and relationship — all captured before the order is placed. Plugs straight into the registrar's downstream systems without the manual reconciliation step you're doing today.

Compliance-ready intake on every order

Program-Specific Waivers

Athletic camps, science labs, field trips, and after-care programs each carry different liability and consent requirements. Checkout extensibility lets you attach the right waiver to the right program SKU — required-to-purchase, stored against the order, e-signed at the point of sale.

"Round up for the AHS scholarship fund" or "Add a $25 / $50 / $100 donation to the annual fund" as a one-click upsell in the checkout flow. Drops directly into your fundraising reporting — separate line item, separate tax treatment, no third-party donation widget needed.

Pricing Model

Why Shopify Offers Variable Platform Fee (VPF)

Plus has two pricing structures — flat monthly (e.g. $2,300/mo) or Variable Platform Fee (VPF), where the platform fee scales with GMV instead of sitting at a fixed rate. Here's why VPF exists and when it's the right call.

The Short Version

VPF aligns Shopify's cost to your business — not the other way around.

Flat-fee SaaS pricing punishes seasonal businesses: you pay the same in November (quiet) as you do in February (peak enrollment). VPF flips that — the platform fee moves with your revenue, so the cost is proportionate to the value you're getting in any given month. As you grow, Shopify wins; in slow months, you're not paying for capacity you're not using.

1. Aligns Cost to Scale

VPF is a percentage of GMV (negotiated by volume tier), with a floor and a ceiling. As GMV grows, the fee scales — but at a lower marginal rate than a flat structure would imply once you cross the ceiling. The merchant and the platform stay aligned on revenue growth.

2. Smooths Seasonal Cost

For a school like AHS with a defined enrollment cycle — November internal promos, January paid push, summer-program peak — VPF means you're not over-paying in the quiet months. Cost-per-dollar-of-GMV stays consistent year-round instead of spiking in slow quarters.

3. Better Economics at Higher GMV

Above certain GMV thresholds, VPF typically nets out cheaper than a flat fee because the volume tiers compound. For merchants in AHS's range with a clear growth trajectory, modeling both side-by-side often makes VPF the obvious call.
