How Shopify gets you there

The levers that grow DTC.

Straight from the audit of worksconnection.com — each of these lifts DTC conversion or captures more direct sales.

Fitment finder (Year/Make/Model + "My Garage")

Today fitment is a buried brand widget — risky for a fitment-critical parts store. A clean vehicle gate (Convermax/PartsPal) means riders only see parts that fit their bike → higher conversion, fewer wrong orders and returns.

Shop Pay + Installments

No express checkout or financing today. Shop Pay = faster, higher-converting checkout; Installments lifts AOV on bigger kits (ties to your "free tee over $150" promo).

Mobile-first

Riders shop on their phones and today's menu reads clunky on mobile. A modern mobile theme converts the audience you already have.

Reviews & trust

No ratings/UGC today. Native reviews build trust and SEO on race-proven parts.

Owned marketing (Klaviyo / Attentive)

No owned email/SMS motion today. Retain and re-sell to a passionate, race-driven rider base — repeat revenue you don't pay a marketplace for.

Dealer self-serve (B2B)

Dealers order via FTP downloads + forms today. Shopify B2B gives them self-serve ordering at their own pricing on the same platform — more wholesale captured online, less manual work.
