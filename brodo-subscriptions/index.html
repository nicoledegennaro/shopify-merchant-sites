Your hesitations — answered honestly

Everything you and Nathan flagged on the call, the reality behind each, and how it actually works on Shopify. Where a claim needs proof, we prove it on your data — not hand-waving.

! "We'll get dinged a transaction fee per subscription, on top of processing."
The reality
This is the assumption driving your "Shopify is expensive" math — and it's tied to Recharge specifically, not Shopify. You pay $0 sub fees on Woo today; you can keep that.
How it works on Shopify
Native Shopify Subscriptions = $0/mo, $0 per transaction. Only standard card processing applies. The Recharge-style stacked fee only exists if you choose Recharge. Loop is a low 1% with no per-order fee if you want its advanced UX.
! "Shopify only wins if we get higher conversion — otherwise it's just more expensive."
The reality
Fair — and you shouldn't take conversion on faith. But the cost side isn't list-price vs list-price: your biggest Woo expense is Nathan's development time, not fees.
How it works on Shopify
We model all-in TCO (hosting + plugins + dev/maintenance + processing) vs Plus, then stack conversion on top as upside. Consolidating your stack and cutting custom-maintenance dev is a real, non-speculative saving before a single conversion point.
! "DTC companies get caught in a tangle of paid plugins — lots is free on Woo."
The reality
Legitimate risk if you rebuild your Woo stack app-for-app. But "free on Woo" still costs you hosting, security, and the dev time to wire it together.
How it works on Shopify
Much is native: checkout, hosting/CDN, PCI, Flow automation (replaces many AutomateWoo jobs), Shopify Email, analytics — all included. Typically ~80% native, ~20% apps. We'll map each current tool (Metric, forms, landing pages) to native-or-app so there are no surprises.
! "Customers modify subscriptions constantly — swap flavors, add/remove, change frequency, pause."
The reality
This is your highest bar, and native's self-serve swap/build-a-box is genuinely lighter than what you run in AutomateWoo today.
How it works on Shopify
Loop (or Skio) does build-a-box, multi-product swaps, frequency, pause, and surveys out of the box. Or extend native via the Subscription API as a custom build (Nathan's wheelhouse) at $0 fee. Monday's deep-dive validates your exact flows live.
! "We get extraordinary concierge service on Woo — we don't want to lose that."
The reality
Real and worth protecting. Service, though, doesn't fix a platform you have to stitch together and patch yourselves.
How it works on Shopify
Plus includes a dedicated Merchant Success Manager, a launch engineer, and 24/7 priority support, plus betas/community — the partnership level you value, without the maintenance burden.
! "Migrating our subscriber data is risky — we'd want it done robustly."
The reality
Agreed, and you're right to want an expert. The sensitive piece is subscriber + payment-token migration.
How it works on Shopify
Payment tokens transfer processor-to-processor — subscribers don't re-enter cards. Woo→Shopify is one of the most common migrations, with mature tooling and 301 redirects. Partner-led, phased, fixed-scope — matching your appetite for near-term fixed cost over long-term variable cost.
