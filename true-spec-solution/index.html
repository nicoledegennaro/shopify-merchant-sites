New in Shopify POS (Spring '26) — built to grow in-store revenue
Endless aisle / ship-to-customer — sell any shaft, head, or club from the full catalog and ship it when a location is out of stock. Capture the sale instead of losing it to "we don't carry that here."
Quick Sale — payment links + shipping — the post-fitting payment link (Matt's #1) now takes payment and shipping in one flow, closing deals after the customer leaves.
Fastest-ever POS (v11) — saves 60+ seconds per checkout / customer-create; more fittings per fitter per day = more revenue capacity at peak.
Tap to Pay across locations (Plus) — take contactless payment on a phone anywhere on the floor at all 41 locations, and trim the hardware bill (no reader needed for a tap).
Online → in-person via Shop app — route local Shop shoppers to the nearest location for pickup and returns; turn online demand into store visits.
Multi-entity across retail (Plus) — run all four brands' locations from one store; the consolidation that removes the reconciliation drag.
