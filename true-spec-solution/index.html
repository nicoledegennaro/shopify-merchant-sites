Why Shopify POS for True Spec
Native spec capture on POS line items (length, swing weight, CPM, adapter, weights) — no Google-sheet workaround.
~73s faster per complex cart (POS v11) — throughput, not friction.
Text/email payment link in <30s — recover deals lost after the fitting.
Fitting + checkout in one system — ends phantom POs, double-ships, manual reconciliation.
Lower card rates vs. your external payment portal.
One source of truth on the customer + cleaner reporting.
Why Plus is the right fit for True Spec: POS Pro is included on Plus at every location — à la carte that's ~$89/mo per store (~$44K/yr across your 41 locations), so the $2,300/mo Plus license already covers POS everywhere, at the lowest card rates. True Spec is already on Shopify Advanced, so the move to Plus is familiar and low-lift — same platform, more included.

Typical implementation & phased rollout
1 · Upgrade Advanced → Plus — True Spec is already on Shopify, so it's a familiar, low-lift upgrade that unlocks POS Pro across your locations.
2 · Stand up POS for fitting — spec capture, payment links, Avalara; read-only sync to ADS / SKU Vault. ADS keeps running.
3 · Pilot a few locations — run side-by-side with ADS to validate the fitter experience.
4 · Phased rollout — extend POS across all 41 locations at your own pace.
