Centered on the problem statement you laid out — the cost of running fitting on a custom POS front-end, how Shopify POS solves it with ADS staying as your ERP, and what it costs.

01 The problem — a custom POS front-end you own forever

True Spec's fitting runs on a custom-built POS front-end, with ADS as the ERP / system of record behind it (your Shopify Advanced store is just merch). ADS exists for one reason: years ago Shopify POS couldn't capture fitting specs on line items, so fitters were stuck in Google Sheets and you built your own front-end to escape it. That custom layer now carries ongoing cost and risk:

Every POS upgrade is yours to build — improving the fitting/checkout experience is custom dev work, on your timeline and budget. Tech debt compounds year over year.
You miss the platform's POS innovation — payment links, Tap to Pay, endless aisle, the Spring '26 updates — each is something you'd have to build yourself, if at all.
No native post-fitting payment link — deals slip when a fitter can't reach a customer by phone after the fitting.
Security & PCI on the payment path sit with you — a custom checkout is yours to patch, certify, and keep compliant.
02 How we'd solve it — modern POS up front, ADS still your ERP

The reason you left is gone: Shopify POS now captures custom specs natively on line items — Chris Cote's Golf runs exactly this fitting workflow today. Put Shopify POS on the fitting front-end and keep ADS as your system of record — in tandem, not a rip-and-replace. Native today, no custom build:

Build-spec capture on line items — length, swing weight, CPM, adapter settings flow to your build team with no price impact. The original blocker, solved (POS v11 ~73s faster per complex cart).
Text/email payment link in <30s — the one you flagged first: the customer pays from their phone, recovering post-fitting deals that slip today.
Fitter + location attribution — every sale logs to the studio and the fitter for commission & accounting reporting, natively — replacing the manual ADS step.
Auto studio → HQ fulfillment routing — the studio rings the sale and the order ships from Scottsdale automatically. No more double-ships.
Gift cards, physical + digital — one unified balance across online + in-store (your site is mostly gift cards today).
Required "how'd you hear about us" field — your marketing gate, native at checkout. Plus a platform that ships upgrades, security, and new features for you.
Why Plus, and the honest catch: POS Pro is included on Plus at every location — à la carte ~$89/mo per store (~$44K/yr across 41) — so the $2,300/mo license covers POS everywhere, plus upgrades, security, and the lowest card rates. The real build is a custom ADS↔Shopify integration (inventory in, orders/payments back to ADS) — a scoped one-time dev cost, included below. Phased and non-disruptive: stand up POS for fitting → pilot a few locations side-by-side → roll out across all 41.
+ New in Spring '26 POS — included, not custom-built
Endless aisle / ship-to-customer — sell any club, head, or shaft from the full catalog and ship it when a location's out of stock. Capture the walk-out sale.
Tap to Pay across locations — contactless payment on a phone anywhere on the floor; also trims hardware.
Online → in-person via Shop app — send local Shop shoppers to the nearest store; online demand becomes foot traffic.
Returns & exchanges in one cart — refund, exchange, and new sale together, keeping the customer in the chair.
~ What we'd scope together — the honest gaps
The configurator (70k+ combinations) — a product-configurator app writes each build to a single line item with its spec; it won't map to native variants.
Appointment / booking — a booking app; today bookings live in ADS and feed the prescription.
Intercompany / on-account billing across the brands — maps toward Shopify B2B (company profiles, payment terms); needs architecture scoping.
Expedite fees that scale by club count — handled with Shopify Functions (developer-level, not native config).
"Phantom orders" — root cause is that ShipStation can't write back to Shopify; resolved once fulfillment runs through Shopify.
