Your problem statement, solved.

A Shopify solution mapped to the four-to-five pinch points you and Matt raised across our June 3, 15, and 22 sessions — built so nothing that works today gets disrupted.

Parent: EB Golf Media LLC — True Spec Golf (B&M custom fitting: 41 US locations, ~$19–20M retail, 9 builders) + Fairway Jockey, Mirror Golf & McLaren Golf (already on Shopify Advanced).
Today's fitting/POS stack: ADS → SKU Vault → Salesforce → ShipStation + Avalara + external payment portal.
Our framing: not "rip out ADS" — solve the pinch points and unify the four brands, with ADS untouched until a proof-of-concept earns the change.
Your problem statement → the solution

1. Fitter productivity & native spec capture

PainSpecs (length, swing weight, CPM, adapter, movable weights) are the heart of the business. Manual entry into Sheets + Shopify caused errors — the reason ADS exists. Anything slowing the fitter slows revenue.

SolutionShopify POS now captures spec data natively on line items — one screen, no Sheets. Spring '26 POS v11 is ~73s faster per complex cart. Chris Cote's Golf runs this exact workflow today.

2. No post-fitting payment link (Matt's #1)

PainFitters lose deals when they can't reach a customer by phone after a fitting; ADS can't send a payment link.

SolutionShopify POS sends a text/email payment link in under 30 seconds; the customer pays on their phone via Shop Pay — recovering deals that slip today.

3. The operational-complexity tax

Pain4+ systems × 4 brands → thousands of phantom open POs, SKU Vault not flowing back to Shopify, manual split-shipments, double-ships, constant reconciliation, no single customer source of truth.

SolutionConsolidate all four brands into one Shopify admin (Spring '26 Multi-Entity Selling), per-location rules/staff/bank accounts, Avalara native. Cote's saw ~50% less custom-order time, ~10 hrs/wk back.

4. Don't disrupt what works

PainADS works. The team isn't looking for another long redesign.

SolutionA self-contained Boston proof-of-concept with read-only stubs to ADS, SKU Vault & Salesforce (inventory sync; ADS order → Shopify draft order for payment). Nothing in today's flow changes during the POC.

5. Four brands & the online custom-club ambition

PainBrands are siloed, and selling custom clubs online has stalled over Fairway Jockey cannibalization concerns.

SolutionOne unified admin gives shared customer data & reporting, a controlled path to sell custom clubs online without cannibalizing Fairway Jockey, plus Shop App reorders and Shopify Audiences (Plus) for higher-intent acquisition.

Addressing what came up on the demo
Hardware across 41 locations: POS is a tablet app, but the POC needs only a handful of devices in Boston — not all 55–58 fitters on day one. We'll cost low-cost tablets (and a BYOD path) explicitly in the TCO, not assume iPads everywhere.
ADS integration: inventory syncs via API from SKU Vault; ADS orders flow into Shopify as draft orders via webhook for payment/invoicing; Avalara stays for tax. Shelby is researching middleware options.
"Worth it at an 89% close rate?": agreed — the payment-link gap is a small slice. The real value is consolidation: unifying four brands, enabling online custom-club sales, cleaner reporting, and removing manual reconciliation.
