Centered on the problem statement you and Matt laid out — what's slowing True Spec's fitting operation, how Shopify POS solves it, and what it costs.

01 The problem

True Spec runs 41 fitting locations — online on Shopify Advanced, in-store on ADS. The fitting workflow is the business, and today's stack puts friction right where revenue happens:

Spec capture lives off-system — fitters log custom specs (length, swing weight, CPM, adapter, weights) in Google Sheets, then re-key them. Errors creep in; it's the reason ADS was built.
A dated, stitched-together stack — ADS + SKU Vault + Salesforce + ShipStation + Avalara + an external payment portal → phantom POs, manual split-shipments, double-ships, constant reconciliation.
No single source of truth on the customer across those systems.
No post-fitting payment link — deals slip when a fitter can't reach a customer by phone after the fitting.
02 How we'd solve it
Native spec capture on POS line items — the Google-sheet workaround disappears (Chris Cote's Golf runs exactly this on Shopify POS), and POS v11 is ~73s faster per complex cart.
Fitting + checkout in one system — ends phantom POs, double-ships, and manual reconciliation; one customer record, cleaner reporting.
Text/email payment link in <30s — recover the post-fitting deals that slip today.
Lower card rates + POS Pro on every location — the cost wins, below.
Why Plus, and why it's low-lift: POS Pro is included on Plus at every location — à la carte that's ~$89/mo per store (~$44K/yr across your 41), so the $2,300/mo Plus license already covers POS everywhere at the lowest card rates. And True Spec is already on Shopify Advanced, so it's a familiar upgrade — same platform, more included. ADS keeps running throughout; the migration is phased: upgrade Advanced → Plus → stand up POS for fitting (read-only sync to ADS / SKU Vault) → pilot a few locations side-by-side → roll out across all 41 at your pace.
+ New in Spring '26 POS — growth on top
Endless aisle / ship-to-customer — sell any club, head, or shaft from the full catalog and ship it when a location's out of stock. Capture the walk-out sale.
Tap to Pay across locations — contactless payment on a phone anywhere on the floor; also trims the hardware bill.
Online → in-person via Shop app — send local Shop shoppers to the nearest store; online demand becomes foot traffic.
Returns & exchanges in one cart — handle a refund, exchange, and new sale together, keeping the customer in the chair.
