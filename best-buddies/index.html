Recommendation

For a single pop-up

If Best Buddies will take donations or process meaningful card volume, NPO Full + POS Pro is the better long-term home (0% third-party fees, donation tools). If the priority is a low-cost, fully-outfitted launch, Grow + the Retail (ToS) offer covers hardware, migration, and ~3 months of POS Pro. Either way it's under ~$2,000/yr for a single location — very manageable for a pop-up.
