Plan & POS pricing — two ways to run retail + online

A side-by-side of Shopify Plus + POS Pro vs. Grow + POS Pro, so you can pick the right fit for your budget and footprint.

The two options

Plus + POS Pro vs. Grow + POS Pro

Both give you full omnichannel retail (in-store POS Pro + online store) on one platform. The difference is cost structure, card rates, and enterprise features.

Proposed deal

Grow + POS Pro

$79 /mo + $89/mo per location
  • POS Pro billed per retail location (+$89/mo each)
  • In-person card rate 2.5% + 10¢
  • Online card rate 2.7% + 30¢
  • Third-party gateway fee 1.0%
  • Full POS Pro feature set (staff roles, exchanges anywhere, advanced inventory, retail reporting)
Best if: budget-focused, a smaller number of locations, standard retail + online needs.
Full Plus

Shopify Plus + POS Pro

from $2,300 /mo (billed annually)
  • POS Pro included across all locations (no per-location fee)
  • Lowest card rates — 2.25% + 30¢ online (lower in-person)
  • Third-party gateway fee just 0.2%
  • Checkout customization, B2B, expansion stores, 10× API
  • Dedicated support & launch engineering
Best if: many locations, higher processing volume, or you need Plus-only capabilities.

The read for a nonprofit

Which one fits Best Buddies

For most nonprofit retail footprints, Grow + POS Pro is the budget-smart choice — you get the full POS Pro feature set and a modern online store at a fraction of the flat Plus fee. Plus becomes worth it when any of these are true:

  • You operate roughly 25+ retail locations (POS Pro included beats per-location fees)
  • You process high card volume where the lower Plus rates (and 0.2% vs 1.0% third-party fee) save materially
  • You need checkout customization (e.g., donation add-ons, round-up, custom flows), B2B, or dedicated support
