What we're solving

The move, in a nutshell.

Why you're leaving Salesforce

  • B2B store restricts B2C features — guest checkout, promo-code flexibility
  • Merchandising (kit comparison charts) needs slow, costly custom dev
  • Frontend is too rigid — drives support-call volume during the July–Sept surge
  • No native punch-out for district procurement (SAP Ariba)

What stays the same

  • NetSuite remains your source of truth for phone & email orders
  • District → school account structure & B2B pricing logic preserved
  • Data keeps flowing to your data lake → PowerBI (reporting + commissions)
  • Shopify becomes the flexible storefront + checkout on top
