Payments · the key decision

Your two payment paths

Because Rx is a regulated, high-risk category, payments run one of two ways. The first step is to submit for approval — that tells us which path you're on.

Option A · if approved

Shopify Payments — Restricted Categories

2.25% + 30¢ + 1% high-risk premium
  • Approved via Shopify Payments' Restricted Categories (pharma) underwriting
  • Visa / Mastercard / Amex only
  • Shop Pay included — one integrated processor, PCI & chargebacks handled
  • Requires supportability review / approval
Option B · fallback

Third-party gateway — Authorize.net

0.20% Shopify fee + processor fees
  • If not approved for Restricted Payments, use Authorize.net via a high-risk merchant account
  • Shopify Plus third-party transaction fee (0.20%) plus Authorize.net gateway + your processor's rates
  • No Shop Pay (only available on Shopify Payments)
  • Faster to stand up, but higher blended cost
Next move on payments: submit for the Restricted Categories review. If approved, you get Shopify Payments + Shop Pay at +1%; if not, Authorize.net is the ready fallback. Either way you can launch — this just decides the rate and whether Shop Pay is on the table. Exact rates confirmed at underwriting / with your processor.
