The strategic role

Online is your acquisition channel

Revenue runs through your reps, but the website is how new customers discover JJ Buckley and experience the wine-storage relationship. A stronger storefront feeds the rep-led business — it's growth, not just transactions.

The real question

Forward cost, not sunk cost

You've invested a decade into a bespoke site and a custom NetSuite. The decision isn't about protecting that — it's whether maintaining an aging custom stack (and its key-person risk) costs more going forward than building on a maintained platform with modern building blocks.

One move

Two brands, together

JJ Buckley (online-only DTC) and SFWTC (adds in-store retail/POS) migrate as one. NetSuite stays your system of record throughout.

The foundation

Shopify Plus as the base

Your most complex needs — including the wine-storage flow — aren't out-of-the-box anywhere. Plus provides the modern primitives to build them, plus a best-in-class storefront and checkout for the acquisition side.

The through-line: keep NetSuite and your rep-led model exactly as they are — modernize the customer-facing layer that drives acquisition, on a platform you're not maintaining alone.

What we're solving

The core requirements — at a business level. The technical build path is what the discovery phase validates.

The differentiator

Wine storage & mix-and-match withdrawal

Customers store wine with you, then withdraw a custom mix across their holdings in one click — owned wine drops into the cart, new purchases add alongside, and it ships as one order with NetSuite kept in sync behind the scenes. This is the flow that has to work, and it's the heart of the build.

Non-negotiable

NetSuite stays the system of record

Rep-entered orders (~95% of sales) keep flowing through NetSuite untouched. The new storefront integrates with it — it doesn't replace it.

Two brands

JJB online + SFWTC retail

Both storefronts migrate together, including SFWTC's in-store POS.

Regulated DTC

Wine compliance built in

Age verification, ShipCompliant/tax, and multi-state DTC wine shipping handled as part of the solution.

Honest framing: this needs real custom work — but so does your current stack. The difference is building it on modern, maintained primitives (Storefront API, checkout & cart extensibility, metafields, webhooks) instead of a one-off codebase.
