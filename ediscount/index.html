On the "unpredictable" question, straight: yes, the fee scales with sales (0.35%) — it's not flat. But at ~$10M / ~$800K+ a month, you're at or above the $2,300 floor nearly every month, so in practice it behaves like a predictable minimum with a known annual total (~$33K). Budget from the year, not the month. And unlike BigCommerce's enterprise re-quote, it's a published formula capped at $40K/mo — no January renegotiation. What we'll walk through together.

So the session is useful for your developers, here's the agenda. If your team can bring notes on any of these ahead of time, we'll get through them faster — but none of it is homework, we can work through it live.

  • What you've custom-built on BigCommerce — each piece, and who maintains it.

  • Themed or headless storefront today — and which front-end framework.

  • Your integrations: ERP / PIM, email/ESP, reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, tax, search, OMS.

  • Peak throughput & API call volumes — and any real-time sync requirements.

  • Your data model: custom fields / attributes that need to map to metafields.

  • Fulfillment / warehouse connections & shipping logic.

Also worth a quick look: who processes your payments today and your current card rates — not covered on our first call. It lets us compare payments side by side (including the extra transaction fee BigCommerce adds unless you're on its own payment rate), so the cost picture is complete, not just the subscription.
