Variable Platform Fee — and your peak months

You pay the higher of the $2,300 base or the VPF on that month's revenue. VPF (online) is 0.35%, and only bites when a month's revenue exceeds ~$657K. At ~$4.5M/yr you're on the base almost every month — the one to watch is a Black Friday / holiday spike.
