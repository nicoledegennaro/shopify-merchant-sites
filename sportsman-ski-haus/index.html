Works with the tools you already use

Shopify is built to be the storefront and checkout layer on top of your existing systems. Here's how it connects to each of the three you asked about. (Exact connection details are confirmed together with your IT team and each vendor.)

Point of Sale & Inventory

Celerant

What it does
Keeps products, pricing, inventory, orders, and customers in sync between your stores and your Shopify online store — one source of truth, no double entry, no overselling.
How it connects
Through Celerant's integration APIs and a connector/middleware layer that links Celerant to Shopify. Your IT team and Celerant confirm the exact connector and sync cadence.
The payoff
Sell in-store and online from the same live inventory, with orders and customer data flowing to the systems you already run.
Email & SMS Marketing

Klaviyo

What it does
Powers your email and text marketing — automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back), segmentation, and reporting driven by real store data.
How it connects
Klaviyo has one of the deepest, official Shopify integrations — a one-click connect. Customers, orders, and on-site behavior sync in real time, no custom build required.
The payoff
More targeted, automated marketing tied directly to what shoppers browse and buy — a proven revenue driver on Shopify.
Local Inventory & Store Traffic

Locally

What it does
Connects the brands you carry to shoppers near your stores — showing "available near you" and enabling find-in-store and pickup, turning brand demand into local foot traffic.
How it connects
Shopify feeds your live inventory to Locally so your in-store stock stays accurate on brand locators. Your IT team confirms the feed/connector setup with Locally.
The payoff
Capture local shoppers searching the brands you sell, and bring them into your stores — online and offline working together.
