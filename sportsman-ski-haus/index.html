Project overview

The idea: use Shopify to modernize how you sell — in-store, online, or both — sitting on top of the systems your team already relies on. There are three sensible ways to approach it depending on how far you want to go. The goal of this meeting is to look at your setup with your IT team and pick the right path.

Start with POS in your stores

Modernize the in-person experience first — Shopify POS as your register across locations — with an online store as a fast-follow whenever you're ready.

Runs on Shopify
In-store POS, unified inventory across all locations, staff tools, customer profiles.
Stays as-is
Your current website (for now); Celerant either retired in-store or bridged while you transition.
Apps
Klaviyo captures customers at the register; Locally publishes your in-store stock to nearby shoppers.
Pricing
Any plan + POS Pro ($89/location) — or Plus, where POS Pro is included across locations.
Best if: the priority is fixing the in-store / POS experience first, before touching the website.

All-in on Shopify — online + POS

One platform end to end: a new Shopify online store and Shopify POS in every location, sharing one inventory, customer list, and reporting.

Runs on Shopify
Website + Shop Pay checkout, in-store POS, inventory, customers, reporting — all unified.
Stays as-is
Celerant retired for commerce, or reduced to a back-office role; data migrated over.
Apps
Klaviyo (native) and Locally (supported) connect directly; Celerant data is migrated in.
Pricing
Advanced or Plus. Plus is favored for multi-store — POS Pro included, best card rates, 10× API limits.
Best if: you want a single system for everything and are ready to move off Celerant for commerce. Biggest lift, cleanest end state.

Shopify online, Celerant stays the register

Shopify powers a modern ecommerce site with Shop Pay; Celerant remains your in-store POS. The two are integrated so inventory and orders stay in sync.

Runs on Shopify
Website + online checkout (Shop Pay), online catalog and customers.
Stays on Celerant
In-store POS and inventory master — unchanged for your store teams.
Apps
The Celerant ↔ Shopify integration is the key piece (API / middleware). Klaviyo & Locally connect to the Shopify side.
Pricing
A standard online plan (Basic / Grow / Advanced) — no POS Pro needed. Lower monthly, but you keep Celerant and maintain the integration.
Best if: you're not ready to replace Celerant in-store and mainly want a better website + Shop Pay.
Agenda · 03

Connecting to your apps

Most apps install from the Shopify App Store, connect to your store once, and then work across both your online store and Shopify POS — the same product, inventory, and customer data flows to them either way. Here's the straight answer on each of the three you asked about, including a ready-made connector vs. an integration to scope, and pricing. Final details are confirmed with your IT team and each vendor.

Klaviyo

Email & SMS marketing
✓ Native connector — one click
Connector
Yes. Klaviyo has an official, one-click integration on the Shopify App Store — one of the deepest on the platform. No custom build.
How it works
Online: customers, orders, and browsing behavior sync in real time to power automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back), segmentation, and reporting. In-store (POS): when staff attach a customer to a sale, that shopper and their in-store purchases sync too — so you capture emails/texts at the register and market to in-store buyers.
Pricing
Free to install, and free up to 250 contacts / 500 emails per month. Paid email starts around $20/mo (up to 500 contacts) and scales with your contact count (~$45/mo at 1,000); SMS from ~$15/mo. Billed by Klaviyo, separate from your Shopify invoice.
View on the Shopify App Store →

Locally

Local inventory & store traffic
✓ Supported integration
Connector
Yes — through Locally's own SyncLocally integration (not a standard App Store app). It connects Shopify to your Locally catalog and embeds the Locally product locator on your pages.
How it works
Online: embeds "available near you" / find-in-store on product pages and can power store pickup and same-day delivery. In-store (POS): Locally publishes the live inventory your POS tracks, so your stores' real stock shows to nearby shoppers — no full online store required.
Pricing
Locally is a subscription platform with quote-based pricing (often partly funded by the brands you carry). Confirm current pricing with Locally directly.
How Locally connects to Shopify →

Celerant

Point of sale & inventory / ERP
⚠ Integration project — no native app
Connector
There isn't a plug-and-play Shopify app for Celerant today. Connecting the two is an integration built on Celerant's APIs, typically through a connector or middleware layer — a scoped project, not a one-click install.
What it would do
Keep products, pricing, inventory, orders, and customers in sync between Celerant and Shopify — one source of truth, no double entry, no overselling.
How it works
Online & POS: the integration syncs products, pricing, and inventory both ways, so whether a sale happens in-store or online, stock and orders stay aligned between Celerant and Shopify.
Pricing
No App Store subscription — because it's a custom integration, the cost is the build / middleware (scoped with Celerant and your IT team), not a monthly app fee. Not listed on the Shopify App Store.
Next step
The main piece to scope with Celerant and your IT team — approach, connector, and sync cadence. It's a well-trodden type of integration; we'll confirm the cleanest path for your setup.

Shopify POS

POS runs on any plan. There are two tiers — a multi-store retailer needs POS Pro.

POS Lite

Free — included in every plan
  • Basic in-person selling on any device
  • Simple customer profiles
  • Returns at the original location only
  • Limited staff access

POS Pro

+ $89 / month per location
  • Unlimited staff logins + roles & permissions
  • Returns & exchanges at any location
  • Advanced inventory — stocktakes, transfers, purchase orders
  • In-store analytics & omnichannel (BOPIS, ship-from-store)
Worth modeling for multi-store

Shopify Plus

from $2,300 / month (billed annually)
Here's the POS math: on standard plans, POS Pro is $89/month per location — with several stores that adds up fast. On Plus, POS Pro is included across all locations, along with the lowest card rates, which is why Plus often pencils out for multi-store retailers like yours. For higher volume, pricing can also move to a revenue-based model.
  • POS Pro included across all locations
  • Up to 10 stores under one plan — multiple banners & locations
  • Lowest card rates — 2.25% + 30¢ (2.9% Amex)
  • 10× higher API limits — for the Celerant integration
  • B2B / wholesale built in
  • Dedicated launch & merchant-success support
