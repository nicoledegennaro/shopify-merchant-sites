Project overview

The idea: use Shopify as the modern storefront and checkout across your retail stores, sitting on top of the systems your team already relies on. Shopify handles the online buying experience — fast pages, one-click Shop Pay checkout, and unified inventory — while your point of sale, marketing, and local-inventory tools keep doing what they do today. The goal of this meeting is to look at your specific setup with your IT team and map out what that would take.

Agenda · 03

Connecting to your apps

Here's the straight answer on each of the three you asked about — including whether there's a ready-made connector or whether it's an integration to scope. Final connection details are confirmed together with your IT team and each vendor.

Klaviyo

Email & SMS marketing
✓ Native connector — one click
Connector
Yes. Klaviyo has an official, one-click integration on the Shopify App Store — one of the deepest on the platform. No custom build.
What syncs
Customers, orders, and on-site behavior flow to Klaviyo in real time, powering automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back), segmentation, and reporting.

Locally

Local inventory & store traffic
✓ Supported integration
Connector
Yes. Locally supports Shopify as an inventory source, so your live stock stays accurate on the brand locators shoppers use to find products near them.
What it does
Shows "available near you" for the brands you carry and enables find-in-store / pickup — turning brand demand into local foot traffic. Your IT team confirms the feed setup with Locally.

Celerant

Point of sale & inventory / ERP
⚠ Integration project — no native app
Connector
There isn't a plug-and-play Shopify app for Celerant today. Connecting the two is an integration built on Celerant's APIs, typically through a connector or middleware layer — a scoped project rather than a one-click install.
What it would do
Keep products, pricing, inventory, orders, and customers in sync between your stores and Shopify — one source of truth, no double entry, no overselling.
Next step
This is the main piece to scope together with Celerant and your IT team — approach, connector, and sync cadence. It's a well-trodden type of integration; we'll confirm the cleanest path for your setup.
