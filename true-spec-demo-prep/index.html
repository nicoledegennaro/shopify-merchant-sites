What we'd like to walk through together.

Every section maps to something specific True Spec shared on the discovery call. None of this is a product roadmap pitch — it's a side-by-side of where ADS works today, where Shopify POS has matured, and what's worth testing.

01

Custom spec capture on POS line items Native

Length, swing weight, CPM, adapter settings, movable weights — captured per line item natively. Every spec field that lives in ADS today maps to a POS attribute, no Google Sheets, no manual re-entry.

02

Attribute-driven pricing via Cart Transform Functions

Expedition fees, head/shaft/grip combo pricing, friends-and-family logic — run natively at checkout rather than as a manual ADS step.

03

Payment link via text or email

Fitter creates a draft order with the full spec sheet, texts a payment link directly to the customer. Customer pays from their phone. Closes the "we fit them, they want to buy, we can't get them on the phone" gap.

04

Per-fitter + per-location attribution POS v11

Fitter and location selected on the order, native commission and budget reporting in Shopify admin — no Salesforce push required to get the reporting out.

05

Cross-location pickup + inventory transfers Spring '26

Studio + HQ split-shipment workflow handled natively in POS now, including itemized transfer manifests. Replaces a meaningful chunk of cross-system reconciliation.

06

Chris Cote's Golf — comparable customer reference

Connecticut-based custom golf fitter, built custom POS apps on Shopify POS for their fitting workflow. 50% reduction in custom club order processing time. ~10 hrs/week saved.

07

Multi-Entity Selling — all 4 brands, one admin Spring '26 · Plus

Operate True Spec, Fairway Jockey, Mirror Golf, and McLaren Golf as separate legal entities under one Shopify store with separate Shopify Payments accounts. Net-new feature that didn't exist 6 months ago.

Scale · what works · where the seams show

Scale

  • 41 domestic locations
  • International partners
  • 9 full-time builders at HQ
  • ~$3.2M inventory in Scottsdale
  • 70K+ spec combinations on True Spec; significantly more on Fairway Jockey

What works

  • ADS handles the custom-spec workflow end-to-end
  • Salesforce is the single source for accounting + commission reporting
  • Avalara handles state-by-state tax
  • Three of four brands already on Shopify Advanced for ecom

Where the seams show

  • "Phantom" orders — ShipStation can't write back to Shopify, orders sit unfulfilled
  • Multi-system reconciliation across 4 brands × 4+ systems
  • Manual split-shipment routing — occasional double-ships
  • No native way to text or email a payment link post-fitting
  • Tax discrepancies between Avalara and Shopify for the ecom brands
What just shipped

Three things in the Spring '26 release that change the picture.

Shopify ships major POS updates twice a year. The Spring '26 "Everywhere" release shipped June 17 — three updates in this release land directly on what True Spec described as friction in the current ADS workflow.

Spring '26 · Plus
Multi-Entity Selling
Operate multiple legal entities — True Spec, Fairway Jockey, Mirror, McLaren — under one Shopify store with separate Shopify Payments accounts. The multi-brand consolidation problem that didn't have a clean answer six months ago has one now.
Spring '26 · POS v11
−73 sec / complex cart
Cart stays visible throughout checkout. Multi-select line items. Dedicated panel for customer + staff attribution. Built for complex carts with custom data — exactly the fitting workflow True Spec runs.
Spring '26
Native transfer manifests
Studio + HQ split-shipment workflow handled inside POS now. Itemized transfer manifests with origin, destination, transfer details. Replaces a meaningful chunk of the SKU Vault + ShipStation reconciliation.
The "why now" piece: Multi-Entity Selling shipped five days ago and is uniquely tailored to True Spec's four-brand structure. It removes the operational tradeoff that previously made the case for staying split across systems.
