Scale · what works · where it breaks

Scale

  • 41 domestic locations
  • International partners
  • 9 full-time builders at HQ
  • $3.2M inventory in Scottsdale
  • 70K+ spec combinations on True Spec, millions on Fairway Jockey

What works

  • ADS handles the custom-spec workflow end-to-end
  • Salesforce is the single source for accounting + commission reporting
  • Avalara handles state-by-state tax
  • 3 of 4 brands already on Shopify Advanced for ecom

Where it breaks

  • "Phantom orders" — ShipStation can't write back to Shopify, orders sit unfulfilled
  • Multi-system reconciliation (4 brands × 4+ systems)
  • Split-shipment errors → occasional double-ships
  • No way to text / email a payment link to a customer post-fitting
  • Tax discrepancies between Avalara + Shopify

Source: True Spec internal walkthrough, June 15, 2026 · Matt Foster, VP Operations

The ask

Good next step — paid Boston POC.

Concrete, scoped, low commitment. The ask that respects Matt's "ADS works, why disrupt it" instinct while giving him real data to evaluate.

Why a paid POC, why Boston

  • Boston has 2 fitters (Brian + Cody) — small enough to control
  • Real fitting workflow, real customer transactions, real spec capture
  • Matt and his developer can stay close to it
  • If POS doesn't beat ADS on the fitter experience, the case dies and you've spent ~6 weeks, not 6 months

Scope

  • One Boston studio configured on Shopify POS
  • Custom POS app for spec capture — Cart Transform Function + line item properties
  • Built by Shopify partner (CQL is obvious fit — Cote's track record)
  • Read-only stubs to ADS / SKU Vault / Salesforce (nothing in production breaks)
  • 4–6 weeks build · 4 weeks live use · structured comparison at the end
Investment
~$25–40K
CQL or comparable partner. Modest. Defensible to leadership as a de-risk before any broader commitment.

Anticipated objections — quick responses

If Matt saysYou say
"We don't see the ROI yet on switching." "Not asking you to switch today. POC on one location, fitters compare side by side. If POS doesn't beat ADS on the fitter experience, the case for switching doesn't get made. We earn it."
"ADS works. Why disrupt it?" "The case isn't 'ADS is broken.' It's 'how many systems are you reconciling.' Across 4 brands × 4+ systems, unification compounds. POC tests whether the unification is worth the disruption."
"Our developer bootstraps in Azure cheap." "Best of both: keep your developer doing what he does well, but on Shopify's API surface — better documented, more stable, unlocks the Plus partner ecosystem when you need it."
"What about the $200K integration cost we'd spend?" "POC on one location is $25–40K, not $200K. Consolidated admin saves on integration cost going forward (no ADS-ShipStation phantom orders to maintain). Net cost is lower over 3 years."
