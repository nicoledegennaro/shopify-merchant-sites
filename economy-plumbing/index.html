Shopify — tailored to Economy Plumbing

A quick primer on how Shopify fits what we discussed, coming from Magento + DDI. The short version: Shopify becomes your modern storefront and checkout, while DDI stays your source of truth.

1

Hosted & fully managed — unlike Magento

No servers to patch, PCI handled, no plugin sprawl. For a lean team, that removes the Magento upkeep and the manual PIM copy-paste you're doing today — Shopify runs the infrastructure so you run the business.

2

Modern storefront + Shop Pay checkout

Fast, mobile-first pages and one-click Shop Pay — the accelerated checkout that lifts conversion, especially on mobile. This is the "streamline & convert" piece you're after.

3

B2B is built in

Company accounts, customer-specific price lists, net terms, and quick reorder — so contractors, GCs, and property managers log in and see their own pricing. Maps directly to the tiered pricing you manage in DDI today.

4

DDI stays your source of truth — and you're not locked in

You're not replacing DDI now — Shopify is the storefront on top of it, with a proven connector (DCKAP) syncing products, inventory, pricing, and orders both ways to end the manual re-keying. And because Shopify is ERP-agnostic, if you migrate off DDI down the road the storefront comes with you — send me your future-ERP shortlist and I'll confirm compatibility.

5

App ecosystem + sales channels

Thousands of apps (marketing, reviews, social) and native channels (social/marketplace) to grow the traffic, referrals, and new-customer acquisition you mentioned — without custom development.

6

Start small, then scale

The curated pilot proves it on a focused set of SKUs without touching your live Magento site or peak operations. Hit the success metrics, then expand to the full catalog — a de-risked path (unlike a big-bang migration).
