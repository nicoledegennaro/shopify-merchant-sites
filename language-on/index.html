If enrollment is approval-gated (deposit → approval → balance)

Since enrollment can hinge on advisor / visa approval, here's how a "deposit now, balance on approval" flow works on Shopify — and three things to get right.

  • Wire the approval to the charge. Shopify's remaining-balance charges are time-based (a set date, or N days after checkout). "Advisor approves → charge the balance" has to be wired via an app or Shopify Flow — doable, but it's a build (an app), not plain admin config.
  • Disclose the full amount at the deposit checkout. Show "$150 today, $X on approval" right in the checkout. A card-on-file charge much larger than the one the student consciously made is the exact pattern issuers dispute — and in strong-authentication markets the later charge only works if the mandate was captured at that first checkout.
  • Design the rejection path first. If approval is denied you're refunding, and in most regions the processing fee doesn't come back. Decide up front whether the deposit is a refundable deposit or a non-refundable application fee, and size it against your real approval rate — a high approval rate keeps this clean; a low one turns it into constant refunds.
Lighter-weight option (good to start): instead of charging a card on file, send a second checkout link once approved — with Shop Pay it's one tap with saved details. You give up ~one tap of friction but drop the mandate, the dispute exposure, and the custom app entirely. Worth knowing the trade-off before building the card-on-file version.
